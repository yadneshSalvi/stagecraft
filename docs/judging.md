# Judge walkthrough

Open [STAGECRAFT](https://stagecraft-swart.vercel.app) and choose **Step inside the production**. Each browser receives its own authenticated private example. Keep the same cookies to return. The fictional film is **THE LAST LIGHT**; sources are synthetic and labelled YouCam results are actual recorded outputs.

## See the complete character

1. Inspect the five-person board. Story days and call order differ. Open **The script** to inspect grounded costume cues; imported unknown chronology is explicitly provisional.
2. Open Nora in **Looks**. Compare the recorded Clothes V4 result with **Original source** and the coat reference. Inspection notes disclose changes such as hem or closure drift.
3. Choose **Makeup detail**. Inspect the separately permitted face and real Makeup VTO result with **Compare makeup**. The recipe is matte lip `#853E5D` at 40 and blush `#9D526C` at 30, smoothing and lip reshaping set to zero. These are separate images, not a composite or evidence of practical application.
4. Record Nora’s **Approve appearance** and **Approve makeup detail** decisions with an explicit reviewer/rationale. Then approve Mae’s costume as an unaffected comparison. Nothing is preapproved. These are synthetic evaluation decisions, not participant research.

## Remove the coat

1. In **Schedule**, solve the allocation. Inspect the solver status, independent checker, grouped evidence gaps and detailed assignments. Adopt to record that operational baseline.
2. From **Production**, choose **Review a change → A garment is unavailable**, `C-014` (oxblood coat). Nora’s scenes 01/04/07 and their three calls are affected. Her costume decision needs renewal; Mae’s costume approval and Nora’s makeup approval remain valid.
3. Select charcoal coat `C-021` and **Adopt this revision**. Direction and historical evidence stay traceable. No appearance decision is made automatically.
4. In Nora’s Costume view, **Use matching recorded result**. This is a genuine earlier output for Ada/charcoal, attached to the current recipe without new provider units. Inspect and approve it. Makeup remains independent.
5. Recompute and adopt allocation. An optimal mathematical assignment does not erase fitting, availability or transport gaps.

## Recast without inheriting evidence

Use a **new example**. The prepared garment matrix includes Celeste with oxblood, not Celeste with charcoal.

1. Review and approve Nora’s original costume and makeup; optionally approve Mae as the unaffected comparison.
2. **Review a change → A character is recast**, Nora → Celeste. Both Nora-specific decisions are affected. Character direction and unrelated evidence remain.
3. Adopt and inspect Celeste’s original body and face. Ada’s outputs and approvals do not transfer.
4. **Use matching recorded result** in Costume and **Use matching recorded makeup** in Makeup detail. The default berry settings match Celeste’s recording. Changed settings correctly fail matching; restore the exact recipe or deliberately generate a fresh output.
5. Review and approve each replacement output independently.

## Hand a frozen book to crew

In **Handoff**, prepare a sourcing draft for the required units. A request, response, confirmation, receipt and return are separate manual records; the synthetic example cannot become a real supplier outcome. No message is sent by the application.

Create a crew pack for selected calls. It freezes the source revision, exact costume and makeup evidence, unit facts, approvals and application notes. Unresolved evidence creates a clearly labelled working pack. Print/save PDF if needed. Change a dependency and revisit the earlier online pack: it shows **Superseded** while retaining its original snapshot. A printed copy cannot update itself.

## Bring your own work

Use **Start your own**, import a permitted text/Fountain script and review the draft before adoption. Add performers through **Cast & sources**, assign uncast script characters, and add private wardrobe references. Body and face uploads have independent permission and capture versions. A new look connects a character, physical unit and scenes. Set call dates separately from story chronology.

Fresh garment generation estimates 2 provider units; makeup estimates 1. Both share app caps of 16 fresh jobs/day and 4 per browser/day. These caps share the upstream account with STEADY. A saved ambiguous submission is never silently retried. **Recover saved request** reuses the original app identifier when a browser receipt is interrupted. Backend and provider retention are separate.
